# Product notes

## Who would use this, and why

| User | Problem today | What StepNotes gives them |
|---|---|---|
| **Beginner student** | Forgets the steps between classes. Videos are too fast and filmed from the wrong angle. | Slow animated footprints from *their own* point of view, spoken counts, mirror mode |
| **Dance teacher** (likely your dancer) | Draws charts by hand or in Illustrator (like the reference images), or sends videos | Make a pattern in a couple of minutes and share it with the whole class |
| **Social dancer** | Learns many figures at workshops and forgets them | A personal "figure notebook" with notes and a video link per figure |
| **Choreographer / performance team** | Needs to write down a routine | Patterns combined into a longer routine (roadmap) |

The strongest first market is probably **teachers → students**. A teacher brings their whole class, which is a natural way for the app to spread.

## Questions to ask your dancer

1. **Who are you making it for, you as a teacher or your students?** This decides whether the editor or the player matters most.
2. Which dances first? Salsa, bachata, ballroom and line dancing each have different needs. For example, ballroom needs *heel / toe / ball* footwork and rise and fall, and line dance needs walls and 32 counts.
3. Do you count "1-2-3, 5-6-7" or "quick-quick-slow"? (The app supports any labels.)
4. Is the **partner view** important (both dancers in one picture, facing each other) or are separate charts enough?
5. What would make you pay? Examples: more patterns, sharing with a class, exporting printable PDFs / images for Instagram, or syncing with music.
6. Is the "weight foot solid, free foot light" convention clear? How do you show taps, pivots and spins on paper today?
7. Would you film yourself and want the footprints shown next to the video?

## Roadmap ideas (rough priority)

**Next**
- Export a chart as an **image / PDF** (for Instagram, WhatsApp groups and class handouts). This is probably the best marketing feature.
- Combine figures into a **routine** (basic → cross-body lead → basic …).
- **Music sync**: play a song and tap along to set the tempo, with a metronome click.
- Footwork details per step: heel / toe / ball / flat, plus a pivot or spin marker (e.g. a 360° icon).
- Show both partners in one combined picture, facing each other.

**Deliberately left out of the current redesign** (the app was kept to a small core: a readable step visualization; these are good candidates once that is proven)
- Practice streaks and practice stats / a progress screen.
- Favourites.
- A settings screen (today the player toggles are the only settings, and they are remembered).
- Count-in before playback starts.
- Tap tempo (tap along to set the bpm).
- Personalised onboarding questions (e.g. "I'm learning / I teach / I choreograph", favourite styles).
- A "Paste" button on the import screen on phones (it needs a clipboard module; today you paste into the field).
- Redo in the editor (undo exists).

**Later**
- Accounts, cloud sync, **share by link**, and a teacher "class" space where students see new patterns.
- A public library or marketplace of patterns from teachers.
- Video next to the diagram, or "trace from video".
- Body / arm / hand-connection hints for leader and follower.
- Localisation (German first? The reference chart is from a German dance studio).

## Monetisation options

- Freemium: free player and up to N own dances; a subscription adds unlimited dances, export and routines.
- Teacher plan: class spaces, branded exports (logo on the chart).
